## 🧭 About me

<img align="right" width="330" src="https://github-readme-stats.vercel.app/api?username=sadmanshkib&show_icons=true&hide_border=true&bg_color=00000000&title_color=38BDF8&icon_color=2DD4BF&text_color=93A5C4&rank_icon=github" alt="Shkib's GitHub stats" />

Final-year **BSc Software Engineering** student at **Yangzhou University** — School of Information Engineering (School of Artificial Intelligence), graduating **June 2027**.

My work sits where pretrained models meet real deliverables: I wire speech recognition, machine translation, text-to-speech and media processing into pipelines that produce finished output files.

Everything I publish is honest about its scope — these are **applied systems** orchestrating pretrained models, no model training and no novel algorithms. What there *is*: deliberate constraint engineering (models chosen to fit a **6 GB GPU**), reliability work (staged, resumable execution), quality control for machine-generated text, and **measurement** — a re-runnable evaluation harness rather than an assumption that it works.

### 🎬 AI Video Dubbing Pipeline
<a href="https://github.com/sadmanshkib/ai-video-dubbing-pipeline"><img alt="license" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Apache--2.0-2DD4BF?style=flat-square&logo=apache&logoColor=white"></a>

End-to-end automatic video dubbing in ten stages: FFmpeg extraction → word-level ASR (Faster-Whisper) → subtitle segmentation → context-aware LLM translation → **duration optimisation** (rewrite each line to fit its time window) → voice generation with optional cloning → timing adjustment (≤ ±5 %) → loudness matching → mux.

`WER 3.42 %` · `BLEU 36.3` · `24× speedup with a 4B model`

### 🌏 Product-Video Localisation · EN → 中文
<a href="https://github.com/sadmanshkib/pyxis_video_dub-pipeline"><img alt="license" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Apache--2.0-2DD4BF?style=flat-square&logo=apache&logoColor=white"></a>

Customer-facing product-video localisation for an industrial water-analysis client. MT alone isn't acceptable for customer material, so this adds **human-in-the-loop review gates**, **adversarial QA passes** that hunt for mistranslation and terminology drift, a persistent **translation memory**, and a tight-pacing export cut.
